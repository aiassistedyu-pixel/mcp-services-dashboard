# Fall Semester Reading List

A shared list of books and articles the team wants to read this semester. Add a note when you finish one.

## Software and design

- *The Design of Everyday Things* by Don Norman — recommended by Maria Lopez for the usability chapter
- *Don't Make Me Think* by Steve Krug — short and practical; good before user testing
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* by Martin Kleppmann — Alex Chen is reading the chapters on indexing and search
- *The Pragmatic Programmer* by Andrew Hunt and David Thomas

## Productivity and teamwork

- *Deep Work* by Cal Newport
- *Atomic Habits* by James Clear — Joe Smith's pick for the book club
- *The Five Dysfunctions of a Team* by Patrick Lencioni

## Fiction for the break

- *Project Hail Mary* by Andy Weir
- *Klara and the Sun* by Kazuo Ishiguro
- *The Left Hand of Darkness* by Ursula K. Le Guin
