# Trip Itinerary: Portland Weekend

A three-day trip for Joe Smith, Maria Lopez, and Alex Chen after midterms. Dates: October 23–25, 2026.

## Travel

- **Train:** Amtrak Cascades, departs Friday 8:05 AM, arrives Portland Union Station 11:40 AM
- **Lodging:** Airbnb in the Pearl District, check-in 3 PM, confirmation #PDX-48213
- **Budget:** about \$250 per person including food

## Friday

- Drop bags at the Airbnb
- Lunch at a food cart pod downtown
- Afternoon at Powell's City of Books (Maria wants the design section)
- Dinner and a walk along the Willamette River waterfront

## Saturday

- Morning hike in Forest Park on the Wildwood Trail (about 5 miles)
- Lunch in the Alberta Arts District
- Afternoon visit to the Portland Japanese Garden
- Evening: board game café; Alex is bringing a Project Falcon demo on his laptop to show friends who live there

## Sunday

- Brunch and coffee tasting
- Saturday Market (open Sunday too) for souvenirs
- Train home at 4:30 PM
