# Recipe: Maria's Black Bean Enchiladas

Maria Lopez brought these to the Project Falcon study night and everyone asked for the recipe. Serves 4 to 6. Prep time 20 minutes, cook time 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 8 medium flour or corn tortillas
- 2 cups red enchilada sauce
- 1½ cups shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- Fresh cilantro, sliced green onions, and sour cream for topping

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly oil a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Sauté the onion and bell pepper in a little olive oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin, and chili powder and cook for 1 more minute.
3. Stir in the black beans and corn. Mash about a quarter of the beans with a fork so the filling holds together.
4. Spread ½ cup of enchilada sauce on the bottom of the dish.
5. Fill each tortilla with filling and a pinch of cheese, roll it up, and place it seam-side down.
6. Pour the remaining sauce over the top and sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until bubbly. Top with cilantro, green onions, and sour cream.

## Tips

- Alex Chen suggests adding a diced chipotle pepper for extra heat.
- Leftovers reheat well for lunch the next day.
- For a vegan version, skip the cheese and sour cream and add avocado.
