# Product Idea: StudySwap

A brainstorm from Alex Chen after a late Project Falcon session.

## The problem

Students buy expensive textbooks and calculators that they use for one semester. Campus marketplaces exist, but they are cluttered, not verified, and full of out-of-date listings.

## The idea

**StudySwap** is a campus-only app for trading, lending, and selling course materials. Users sign in with their school email, so every listing comes from a verified student. Listings are tied to course codes, so searching "MATH 221" shows exactly the books and supplies needed for that class.

## Key features

- School email verification
- Search by course code, ISBN, or professor
- Lend mode with return reminders
- Safe meetup spots suggested on campus
- Ratings for reliable traders

## Target users

- First-year students on a tight budget
- Graduating seniors clearing out their shelves
- Student clubs that share equipment

## Business model

- Free for students
- Optional featured listings for \$1
- Partnerships with the campus bookstore for buyback offers

## Feedback so far

- Joe Smith likes the course-code search and thinks it could reuse the indexing work from Project Falcon.
- Maria Lopez worries about no-shows at meetups and suggests a deposit system.
